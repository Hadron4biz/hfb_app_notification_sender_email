Notification Sender Email

The chatter popover shows who a message really went to - not who the partner record says today.

The problem

Click the envelope icon on a chatter message and Odoo shows you who it was sent to - by reading the partner's email address right now. Change that address later, and every past notification quietly starts showing the new one, as if it had always gone there. The actual delivery history is lost.

How it works

1

A message is sent

The recipient's email address is snapshotted right away.

2

The partner's email changes

Doesn't matter - the snapshot from step 1 stays put.

3

Open the popover, later

It still shows the real address the message went to, plus when it was sent.

Where it applies

Any email notification sent through Odoo's chatter (mail.notification) - invoices, sale orders, any document with a Discuss thread. No new models, no extra configuration, no security rules - install it and future notifications keep their real address for good.
